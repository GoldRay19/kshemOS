# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the KshemOS community a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone.

We expect all contributors and community members to interact professionally and constructively.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

* Treat others with respect and professionalism.
* Be open to different perspectives and experiences.
* Give constructive feedback.
* Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
* Focus on solving problems rather than personal disagreements.
* Respect project maintainers, contributors, and users.
* Protect private and sensitive information.
* Help maintain a safe and welcoming community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Hate speech or discriminatory comments.
* Sexual harassment or inappropriate sexual content.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Deliberate abuse of project infrastructure.
* Malicious behavior intended to harm users or contributors.
* Repeatedly disrupting discussions or collaboration.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all KshemOS community spaces, including:

* GitHub repositories and discussions
* Issues and pull requests
* Community chats and communication channels
* Events or meetings associated with the project

It also applies when an individual is representing the KshemOS project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

If behavior violates these guidelines, maintainers may take appropriate action, including:

1. Asking the person to stop the behavior.
2. Removing inappropriate content.
3. Temporarily restricting participation.
4. Permanently restricting participation in project spaces.

Actions will be determined based on the nature and severity of the violation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the KshemOS project maintainers.

When reporting an incident, provide as much relevant information as possible, including:

* What happened
* When and where it happened
* Who was involved
* Links or screenshots when available

Please avoid publicly posting sensitive information about the incident.

## False Reports

Reports should be made in good faith. Deliberately submitting false or malicious reports may itself be considered a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## Commitment

We encourage everyone involved with KshemOS to contribute to a community built on respect, collaboration, transparency, and responsible technology.

Thank you for helping create a positive environment for everyone.
